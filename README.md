### Project: Spatial Analysis of Free Trade Zones in the Dominican Republic (2000-2016)

Recognizing the need to understand the territorial impact of Free Trade Zones (FTZs) in the Dominican Republic, I developed a unique dataset that links key administrative data with their precise geographical location (Latitude, Longitude, geometry).

#### Methodology and Scope:

* **Data Origin:** Key administrative information extracted from official Free Trade Zone reports (PDFs), covering the period 2000-2016.
* **Variables Included:**
    * **Geographical Details:** Latitude, Longitude, geometry (for visualization and spatial analysis).
    * **Economic Variables:** Accumulated Investment, Expenses (Water, Infotep, Telecommunications, Electricity, TSS), Salaries (Technicians, Operators).
    * **Employment Variables:** Workers (F/M), Technicians (F/M), Administrative Staff (F/M).
    * **Infrastructure Variables:** Constructed Facilities, Occupied Facilities, Number of Companies.
* **Main Achievement:** Successfully georeferenced 74 out of 95 Free Trade Zones that operated between 2000 and 2019, overcoming the historical limitation of missing location data.

#### Impact and Applications:

This dataset facilitates more granular impact analyses, enabling studies at the municipal level or even at a grid level when combined with other technologies such as satellite imagery (e.g., VIIRS night lights data). A preliminary exploratory analysis has already demonstrated the correlation between the presence of Free Trade Zones and municipal economic development.

#### Considerations:

* Special Free Trade Zones were not included.
* Most of the un-georeferenced zones correspond to parks that were closed during the period.
